Everything a small team needs to stay on track

No more deadlines lost in chats and spreadsheets. Everyone sees who owns what and when it is due.

  • ProjectsCreate, edit and archive projects with a description, start date and deadline.
  • Kanban boardDrag tasks across To Do, In Progress, Review and Done.
  • CollaborationComment on tasks and attach files where the work happens.
  • Activity logSee who created, changed or deleted something, and when.
  • Search and filtersFind tasks by keyword, status, assignee, priority or due date.
  • DashboardCharts for tasks by status, overdue work and workload per member.

Clear roles, so ownership is never unclear

RoleWhat they can do
OwnerCreates the organization, manages the plan, invites and removes members, assigns roles.
AdminCreates and archives projects, manages boards, assigns tasks to anyone.
MemberWorks on assigned tasks, updates status, comments and uploads files.
